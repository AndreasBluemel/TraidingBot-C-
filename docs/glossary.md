
The acronym OHLCV stands for the 5 key data points represented in each period:

  -O (Open): The starting price of the asset at the beginning of the selected timeframe (e.g., the start of a day or an hour).

  -H (High): The highest price the asset reached during that period.

  -L (Low): The lowest price the asset reached during that period.

  -C (Close): The final price of the asset at the very end of the timeframe.

  -V (Volume): The total amount of the asset traded (bought and sold) during the period. This is usually displayed as a vertical bar chart right below the candlesticks.

How to Read a Candlestick
A candlestick consists of a body (the thick rectangular part) and wicks or shadows (the thin lines extending from the top and bottom).

The Body: Shows the difference between the Open and Close prices.

The Wicks/Shadows: Show the extreme price fluctuations (the High and the Low) outside of the opening and closing prices.

Green (Bullish) Candle: The price went up. The Close price is higher than the Open price. The bottom of the body is the Open, and the top of the body is the Close.

Red (Bearish) Candle: The price went down. The Close price is lower than the Open price. The top of the body is the Open, and the bottom of the body is the Close.
